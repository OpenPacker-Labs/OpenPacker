# Governance

OpenPacker has one maintainer. It has no foundation, company board, or
committee.

## BDFL

Tyler Kendrick ([@Tyler-R-Kendrick](https://github.com/Tyler-R-Kendrick)) is the
BDFL (benevolent dictator for life). The BDFL accepts or rejects changes to this
repository, including the PREX contracts in this tree, and publishes releases.

## How decisions are made

Propose changes in issues and pull requests. Discussion there informs the
maintainer. The BDFL decides what lands, including when a public contract
changes and when a release ships.

Review expectations are in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). A decision that
needs a durable record is written up as an ADR under
[docs/adr/](docs/adr/README.md) or in the pull request that lands the change.

## Becoming a committer or maintainer

The BDFL may grant commit access, or name an additional maintainer, after a
sustained record of careful contributions and review on this repository. The
BDFL may later narrow or remove that access. Those grants do not create a board
or a committee.

## Dispute resolution

Raise a technical disagreement on the issue or pull request it concerns. If that
discussion does not settle it, the BDFL decides and records the outcome on that
thread or in an ADR.

Conduct reports follow [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Send them
through GitHub private reporting for `Tyler-R-Kendrick/OpenPacker`
(<https://github.com/Tyler-R-Kendrick/OpenPacker/security/advisories/new>)
and/or contact [@Tyler-R-Kendrick](https://github.com/Tyler-R-Kendrick) on
GitHub.

## Releases

The BDFL tags and publishes releases of this repository. What must be true
before a release is the checklist in
[Contributing and releasing](docs/operations/contributing-releasing.md).
Publishing credentials stay outside the repository.

## Continuity

If the BDFL is unavailable, no one else is authorized to publish a release of
`Tyler-R-Kendrick/OpenPacker` or to change this file, unless a successor is
named in this section on the default branch. A successor is named only by a
commit from the BDFL that updates this section. Until then, issues and pull
requests wait, and the last published tree stands.

If no successor is named, anyone may fork the repository under its license and
maintain the fork. This document does not appoint a foundation or a committee to
take over.
